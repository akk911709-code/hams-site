# PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** January 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Thank you for using **Hams** ("the App"), developed by **QIANKUN** ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile browser and resource management application. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect data to provide the App's core functionality (resource downloading), manage user accounts (VIP services), and display advertisements (for non-VIP users).

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us
* **Account Information (Firebase Auth):** If you choose to register or log in, we collect your **email address** and assign a unique **User ID (UID)**.
* **VIP Membership Data:** For paid subscribers, we process and store specific membership details via **RevenueCat**, including:
    * VIP Status and Membership Level
    * Subscription Expiration Date
    * Download Quotas and Usage History
    * Purchase Records (Transaction IDs, but **not** your credit card details, which are handled by Apple/Google).
* **Anonymous Identifiers:** If you use the App without logging in, we may assign an anonymous identifier to manage basic app settings.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
* **Device Sensors (Accelerometers & Gyroscopes):**
    * We access your device's accelerometer and gyroscope sensors **only** when you voluntarily use the "Shake" feature.
    * **Purpose:** This is used solely to trigger specific interactions (e.g., "Shake to Watch Ad").
    * **Data Retention:** Sensor data is processed in real-time locally and is **never stored** or uploaded to our servers.
* **Network Activity & Media Sniffing:**
    * To fulfill the App's core function, the App analyzes URLs visited within the built-in browser to identify downloadable resources (e.g., `.m3u8`, `.mp4`).
    * This processing happens locally. We do not track your browsing history for surveillance purposes.
* **Advertising Data (Google AdMob):**
    * For **non-VIP users**, we collect data regarding ad impressions, clicks, and user interactions.
    * We use the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to manage consent for personalized ads.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data for the following purposes:
1.  **Service Provision:** To process downloads, manage VIP membership via RevenueCat, and sync status across devices.
2.  **Monetization (AdMob):** To display advertisements to free users.
    * **VIP Exemption:** **VIP users are exempt from ad targeting.** If you are a VIP, your data is isolated from the advertising profiling system.
3.  **Functionality:** To enable motion-based features via device sensors.
4.  **Improvement:** To analyze download success rates and optimize App performance.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We employ third-party companies to facilitate our Service. These parties access your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf:

* **Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore & Analytics):** Used for user authentication, secure cloud database, and app analytics. [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **RevenueCat:** Used for processing and managing VIP subscriptions and purchase receipts. [Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
* **Google AdMob:** Used for displaying advertisements (Free users only). [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
* **Google UMP:** Used for gathering user consent for privacy regulations.

## 5. Permissions
The App requests the following permissions to function:

* **Internet Access:** Required to browse the web and download files.
* **Storage / Photos:** Required to save downloaded files to your local device.
* **Sensors (iOS Motion Usage):**
    * On iOS, the App may request permission to access motion data.
    * **Strict Usage:** This permission is requested **only** if you attempt to use the "Shake" feature. It is not used for background tracking.

## 6. Data Security and International Transfer
* **Data Security:** We verify that appropriate security measures are in place to protect your data. All data transmission between the App and our servers uses **HTTPS encryption**. Your cloud data is secured by Google Firebase's enterprise-grade security standards.
* **International Transfer:** As a global application, your information (specifically User ID and Membership Status) may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your state, province, or country (including servers in the United States or Singapore) where data protection laws may differ. We ensure all transfers comply with applicable laws.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion
* **Cloud Sync:** Account data is synchronized across your devices via Firebase.
* **Retention:** We retain your account information as long as your account is active to provide VIP services.
* **Deletion:** You can request account deletion directly within the App settings.
    * **Cloud Data:** Deleting your account will permanently remove your User ID and membership records from Firebase and RevenueCat.
    * **Local Data:** Downloaded files stored locally on your device must be manually deleted by you.

## 8. GDPR & CCPA Compliance
* **For European Users (GDPR):** You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. We use Google UMP to collect consent.
* **For California Users (CCPA):** You have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information via the App settings.

## 9. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for use by children under the age of **13 (or 14 in jurisdictions such as Mainland China)**. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under this age. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.
