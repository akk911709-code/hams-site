# PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** January 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Thank you for using **Hams** ("the App"), developed by **QIANKUN** ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile browser and resource management application. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect data to provide the App's core functionality (resource downloading), manage user accounts (VIP services), and display advertisements (for non-VIP users).

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us
* **Account Information (Firebase Auth):** If you choose to register or log in, we collect your **email address** and assign a unique **User ID (UID)** to manage your membership status, download quotas, and usage history.
* **Anonymous Identifiers:** If you use the App without logging in, we may assign an anonymous identifier to manage basic app settings.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
* **Device Sensors (Accelerometers & Gyroscopes):**
    * We access your device's accelerometer and gyroscope sensors **only** when you are using the "Shake" feature.
    * **Purpose:** This is used solely to trigger specific interactions, such as the "Shake to Watch Ad" feature to unlock temporary benefits.
    * **Retention:** Sensor data is processed in real-time and is **not stored** or uploaded to our servers.
* **Network Activity & Media Sniffing:**
    * To fulfill the App's core function (downloading media), the App analyzes the URLs you visit within the built-in browser (specifically looking for media extensions like `.m3u8`, `.mp4`, images).
    * This processing happens locally on your device to identify downloadable resources. We do not track your browsing history for surveillance purposes.
* **Advertising Data (Google AdMob):**
    * For non-VIP users, we collect data regarding ad impressions, clicks, and user interactions to monetize the App.
    * We use the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to manage your consent regarding personalized ads.

### 2.3 Local Storage & Cache
* **Media Files:** Files you download are stored locally on your device.
* **App Preferences:** We use local storage (e.g., SharedPreferences) to save your settings (resolution preferences, WiFi-only download mode, etc.).
* **Cookies:** The built-in browser may accept cookies from third-party websites you visit. You can clear these at any time via the App's settings.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data for the following purposes:
1.  **Service Provision:** To process your downloads, manage your VIP membership, and sync your status across devices.
2.  **Monetization (AdMob):** To display advertisements to free users. **VIP users are exempt from ad targeting.**
3.  **Functionality:** To enable motion-based features (Shake to Ad) via device sensors.
4.  **Improvement:** To analyze download success rates and optimize App performance.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

* **Google Firebase (Auth & Analytics):** Used for user authentication and infrastructure. [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* **Google AdMob:** Used for displaying advertisements. [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
* **Google UMP:** Used for gathering user consent for privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA).

## 5. Permissions
The App requests the following permissions to function:
* **Internet Access:** Required to browse the web and download files.
* **Network State:** Required to detect if you are on WiFi or Mobile Data (to save data usage).
* **Storage/Photos:** Required to save downloaded files to your device.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
* **Retention:** We retain your account information (Email/UID) as long as your account is active.
* **Deletion:** You can request the deletion of your account and associated data directly within the App settings or by contacting us. Upon deletion, your cloud data (VIP status, history) will be permanently removed. Local files on your device must be deleted manually.

## 7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance
* **For European Users (GDPR):** You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. We use the Google UMP SDK to collect your consent for ad personalization.
* **For California Users (CCPA):** You have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. You can manage your ad preferences via the App settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
